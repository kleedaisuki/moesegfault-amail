## Non-blocking operational conditions

1. **Pin the deployed Worker before any attestation.** The workflow does not itself compare the 100%-serving staging Worker revision with the reviewed v4 source. As the design note requires, the operator must establish this separately; otherwise a failure can be a version mismatch, and a green run cannot be attributed to this source revision with confidence. This is a release/provenance condition, not a defect in the score calculations.
2. **Keep the claim narrow.** The exact oracle's two rows both satisfy mailbox/title/unread; negative predicate behavior is checked by the preceding `semantic_cases`, not by the exact two-page comparison itself. A green result proves exact cosine and pagination for the two-document eligible snapshot, not large-mailbox exhaustiveness or every filter combination. Retain this wording in the eventual live report.

The focused tests mock D1, provider, and CLI boundaries; they validate local rejection and orchestration but are not hosted evidence. No further source change is required by this review.
